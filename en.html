Rahbord Agency at a Glance

Specialist partner for investment attraction, development and international financing

Mission

Transform capacities, assets, ideas and economic plans into investment-ready projects, and establish professional connections between project owners and investors, financiers, executive bodies and domestic and international partners.

Vision

Become one of Iran’s most reputable private investment development and attraction agencies and a professional partner for strategic projects in regional and international engagements.

Value Proposition

Provide an integrated chain of services, from project screening and packaging to negotiation, capital entry permits, aftercare and financing.

Audiences

Companies and holding groups, public organizations, provincial governments and municipalities, free zones, universities, knowledge-based companies and private-sector project owners.

Professional Principles

Transparency, confidentiality, legal compliance, conflict-of-interest management, financial realism, accountability and continuous reporting.

Rahbord Agency does not guarantee investment outcomes; through professional effort, targeted networking and process facilitation, it increases the likelihood of project success.

Rahbord’s Comprehensive Service Cycle

From raw potential to implemented project

1. Identification

Identify capacities, assets, development projects and investment opportunities.

2. Screening

Initial economic, financial, technical, legal and market assessment and project preparation.

3. Preparation

Studies, financial model, valuation, profile, investment memorandum and data room.

4. Structuring

Design partnership models, equity and debt mix, risk allocation and exit mechanisms.

5. Marketing

Define target markets, develop bilingual content, organize events and targeted introductions.

6. Attraction and Negotiation

Investor identification, B2B, preliminary suitability assessment and negotiation management.

7. Legal Facilitation

Support foreign investment licensing, registration of capital entry and institutional coordination.

8. Implementation Support

Monitor commitments, resolve obstacles, develop supply chains and reinvestment.

Rahbord’s Operating Principle

Each project is managed with a designated project manager, operational plan, performance indicator, reporting calendar and dedicated financial contract.

Governance, Compliance & Reporting

Operate within valid licenses and the laws and regulations of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

Verify identity, assess suitability initially and identify the investor’s ultimate beneficial owner.

Conduct preliminary source-of-capital checks and comply with anti-money laundering and anti-corruption requirements.

Manage conflicts of interest and disclose material financial relationships.

Protect information, use confidentiality agreements and tier access to the data room.

Clearly distinguish capital under negotiation, committed, contracted, entered and registered.

Provide periodic project reporting and monitor performance indicators.

No guarantee of outcomes, permits, fund transfers or investment returns.

Engage qualified lawyers, auditors, experts and financial institutions for specialized services.

Professional Trust

Transparency in roles, limitations, costs and risks is the basis of sustainable cooperation among the investor, project owner and Rahbord Agency.
